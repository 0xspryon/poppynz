Helper Verification

Every Mom Helper Is Background-Checked

Required

Government photo ID

What it is. A passport, driver’s licence or provincial photo card, matched to the helper’s face and legal name.

Why we ask. So the person on the profile is the person at your door, and so every other check is tied to a real identity.

Required

Vulnerable Sector Check

What it is. A police check specifically for people who will work with children or vulnerable adults. It includes records a standard check does not.

Why we ask. Childcare and elderly check-ins are exactly the roles this check was designed for.

Required

Enhanced criminal record check

What it is. A national criminal record search, fetched securely through our partner Credibled.

Why we ask. It covers what the Vulnerable Sector Check doesn’t and gives the admin a complete picture.

Optional

First Aid / CPR

What it is. A current certificate from a recognised provider, verified by an admin.

Why we ask. Shown as a tag on the profile so families can choose helpers who hold it.

Optional

Early Childhood Educator (ECE)

What it is. A diploma or provincial registration in early childhood education.

Why we ask. Useful for families seeking structured childcare or tutoring for younger children.

Optional

Personal Support Worker (PSW)

What it is. A PSW certificate from a recognised program.

Why we ask. Relevant for elderly check-ins and families caring for an older relative.

Our background-check partner

Record checks are fetched through Credibled.

Credibled is a Canadian background-screening provider. When a helper requests their checks from inside Poppynz, Credibled runs the enhanced criminal record check and coordinates the Vulnerable Sector Check with the relevant police service. The result is returned to a Poppynz admin, never posted to a profile, never shown to families.

  • Requested by the helper from inside Poppynz, no station visit for the record check
  • Consent is given by the helper each time; nothing is fetched without it
  • Results are seen by a Poppynz admin only, never by families
  • Credibled’s own privacy policy applies to their processing; ours applies to everything else

Reviewed by Real People

Every Approval Is Reviewed by a Real Person

Automated checks confirm that documents are real and match up. Then someone on our team reads the whole file. ID, checks, credentials, profile, and makes the call. If anything is unclear, we simply ask the helper first. The Vetted badge is only ever given by a person.

Referrals work the same way. A member can vouch for someone they know, which opens the door; it does not skip a single check.

  1. 01Documents arriveID, checks and any credentials land in the admin queue.
  2. 02Match and readThe admin confirms names and photos match, and reads each result in full.
  3. 03Ask if unclearAnything ambiguous goes back to the helper as a question, not a rejection.
  4. 04Approve, or notOnly an admin can set the Vetted badge. Approvals are logged with who reviewed and when.

Verified Families

Verified Families. We think helpers deserve that.

A helper is walking into someone’s home, often for the first time. So before a family can find or message anyone, we ask them to complete a short safety check, identity and a few household details. That way helpers only ever hear from verified families.

  • Identity confirmed with a government ID
  • Home address confirmed, it sets the distance helpers see
  • Household details: who’s at home, pets, anything a helper should know
  • Every agreement is signed in writing, by both sides, before the first session

Your Privacy

Your Information Is Safe With Us

End-to-end encrypted messaging

Conversations between families and helpers are encrypted in transit and at rest.

PIPEDA-compliant

We follow Canada’s federal privacy law: collect only what we need, say why, and let you access or delete it.

Passwordless sign-in

A magic link by email. No password to reuse, leak or forget.

Check results stay private

Background-check documents are visible to admins only. Families see the Vetted badge and credential tags, never the files.

Our Promise to You

Things We Will Never Do

  • Approve a helper without a human admin reviewing their file
  • Show a family the contents of a background check
  • Sell or share your personal data with advertisers
  • Let an unverified family message a helper
  • Publish ratings or reviews we haven’t collected, the product has none yet, so you won’t see any
  • Change the rate on a signed agreement
