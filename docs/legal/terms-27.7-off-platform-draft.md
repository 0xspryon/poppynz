## Proposed placement

Section 27 ("General"), after 27.6.

## Proposed wording

**27.7** Poppynz’s protections, including identity and background vetting,
payment protection and dispute support, apply only to communications,
contracts and payments made through the Poppynz platform. If you choose to
communicate, make arrangements or pay outside the platform, you do so at your
own risk, and Poppynz is not responsible for any loss, dispute or harm arising
from those dealings.

## Questions for legal

- Is the limitation of responsibility enforceable as drafted in each province
  where Poppynz operates (consumer-protection statutes, Quebec in particular)?
- Should it cross-reference the payment and cancellation policy?
- Does it need to be presented for fresh acceptance (a new Terms version with
  the acceptance gate) rather than as a silent update?
