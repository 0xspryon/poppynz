Poppynz: Born From a Need, Fueled by Heart

There was no one to call and no magic button to press. So we built one. The founder on why Poppynz exists.

MMombie · Founder, PoppynzPublished 1 Jan 2025
Share

For years, I’ve lived life at full speed, a constant hum of activity, a relentless pursuit of balance that often felt more like a tightrope walk. You know the feeling, that constant go, go, go where every task feels urgent, every responsibility weighs heavy? It’s the life of the sandwich generation, a place where the needs of aging parents and young children collide, amplified by the isolating reality of being an immigrant in a new city/ country, etc. My story isn’t unique. Like so many others, I found myself stretched thin, caught between the demands of caring for my parents and my partner’s parents, all while trying to raise my own family. The dream of a supportive village, the helping hands of grandparents nearby, remained just that – a dream. Distance, illness, and simple unavailability meant that the backup I so desperately craved just wasn’t there. There were days, and nights, that blurred together, a relentless cycle of tasks and to-dos. I longed for a moment of respite, a chance to simply breathe. A chance to shower without feeling like I was abandoning a child, a chance to focus on a work project without the constant worry of a parent’s well-being. I just wanted a sliver of time, a moment to recharge, so I could be the best version of myself – the perfect mom, daughter, sister, entrepreneur, colleague, and friend I so desperately wanted to be.

But the reality was, there was no one to call. No magic button to press for instant help. And in the quiet moments of exhaustion, a thought began to take root: What if there was? What if there was a service, a reliable, compassionate service, that could step in when you needed it most? A service that understood the unique pressures of navigating life without a built-in support system? A service that could provide that much-needed moment of alleviation, that precious window of time to simply be? That’s how Poppynz Inc. was born. It wasn’t a business plan hatched in a boardroom; it was a lifeline forged in the fires of personal experience. It was a response to the quiet desperation of feeling like you’re running on empty, with no refill in sight.

Poppynz is more than just a service; it’s a promise. A promise to provide that support, that helping hand, that moment of respite you deserve. We understand the weight of juggling multiple responsibilities, the ache of longing for family support that isn’t readily available. We know what it feels like to just want a chance to shower, to catch your breath, to feel human again. We’re here to offer that alleviation, that temporary reprieve, so you can step back into your life feeling recharged and ready to tackle whatever comes next. Because you deserve to be more than just a list of tasks and responsibilities. You deserve to be seen, supported, and given the chance to simply be. Poppynz is my way of building the village I so desperately needed, and my hope is that it can be your village too. We’re here to help you keep running on full cylinders, but with a support system that allows you to refuel along the way.
