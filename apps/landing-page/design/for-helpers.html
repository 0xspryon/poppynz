Getting Started

Becoming a Certified Mom Helper Is Simple

  1. You

    Complete Your Profile

    Photo, bio, services and rates. Say what you do in your own words.

  2. You

    Verify Your ID & Background

    Government ID, then request your Vulnerable Sector and record checks through Credibled.

  3. Poppynz admin

    Poppynz Review

    Someone on our team reads your full file. Every approval is reviewed by a person, never automated.

  4. Approved

    Get Certified & Go Live

    Your Vetted badge appears and verified families nearby can find you.

Required

Every helper

  • Government photo ID, passport, driver’s licence or provincial card. You must be 18+.
  • Vulnerable Sector Check, the police check required for working with children and vulnerable adults.
  • Enhanced criminal record check, requested through Credibled from inside the app; you don’t need to visit a station.
Checks are fetched securely via our partner Credibled. Results go to a Poppynz admin, not to families.
Optional

Shown on your profile

  • First Aid / CPR, current certificate from a recognised provider.
  • Early Childhood Educator (ECE), diploma or registration.
  • Personal Support Worker (PSW), certificate, useful for elderly check-ins.

Credentials are verified by an admin and appear as tags on your profile. They’re not required to be approved.
