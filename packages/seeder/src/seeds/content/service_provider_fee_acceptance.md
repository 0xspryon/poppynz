**Poppynz Earning Fee**

Poppynz deducts a service fee equal to **15% of your Earning Fee** from each completed booking. Your estimated payout, before applicable taxes, adjustments, refunds, chargebacks, or other clearly disclosed amounts, is **85% of your total Earning Rate**.

**Example:** If your Earning Rate is $25.00 per hour, the Poppynz service fee is $3.75 per hour and your estimated payout is $21.25 per hour.
