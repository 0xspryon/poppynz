**Poppynz Family Service Fee**

Poppynz charges families a service fee equal to **5% of the Caregiver Charges** for each booking. This fee is added to the amount charged by the caregiver. Any applicable taxes and other charges will be separately displayed before you confirm payment.

**Example:** If the Caregiver Charges are $100.00, the Poppynz Family Service Fee is $5.00. Your subtotal is $105.00, plus any applicable taxes or other charges displayed at checkout.
